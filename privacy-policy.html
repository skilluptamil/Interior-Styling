Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 7, 2026

At HAVEN & HEIR, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and property information entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data handling practices across our staging consultations, furniture rental services, and digital platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide when requesting staging proposals, booking consultations, or renting designer furniture:

  • Personal Identification: Full name, email address, phone number, and real estate brokerage affiliation.
  • Property Records: Listing address, square footage, architectural layout, target listing date, and room dimensions.
  • Transaction Information: Invoicing details, billing address, and payment confirmation processed through secure, encrypted payment providers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes:

  • Preparing bespoke staging proposals, mood boards, and furniture rental agreements.
  • Coordinating white-glove furniture delivery, installation, styling, and post-sale de-staging.
  • Communicating project updates, scheduling photo shoots, and answering inquiries.
  • Complying with property access rules, building management policies, and insurance guidelines.

Our Guarantee: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or property data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Photography & Media Rights

Professional photography is essential to luxury home staging. In connection with our services:

  • Portfolio Documentation: Unless agreed otherwise under a written privacy rider or NDA, HAVEN & HEIR reserves the right to photograph styled spaces for our digital portfolio and social media.
  • Homeowner Privacy: We protect homeowner anonymity. Public portfolio displays never disclose homeowner names, unit security details, or sensitive personal items.

4. Information Sharing & Security

We only share necessary information with bonded freight/transport teams required to deliver staging furniture and secure cloud infrastructure providers. We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.3) and strict access controls to safeguard all records against unauthorized access.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal information, update or correct records, request data deletion (subject to legal retention requirements), and opt out of marketing communications at any time.
