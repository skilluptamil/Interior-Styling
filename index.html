Seamless Methodology

Our 4-Step Staging Process

From initial spatial evaluation to the final champagne-ready reveal, our proven process is designed for effortless execution and maximum return.

01

Consultation

We visit your property for an in-depth spatial assessment, analyzing architecture, natural light, target buyer demographics, and marketing objectives.

02

Design & Planning

Our stylists create a bespoke mood board and floorplan, selecting custom designer furniture, art, rugs, lighting, and textiles from our private warehouse.

03

Staging & Styling

Our white-glove logistics team delivers and installs every piece. Our master stylists style vignettes, fluff pillows, and perfect every sightline.

04

Final Presentation

Your property is 100% photo-ready and primed for open houses. When sold, we handle complete, prompt de-staging without any hassle.
