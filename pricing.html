Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can HAVEN & HEIR stage my property?

Once the staging proposal and deposit are confirmed, our team typically installs within 48 to 72 hours, completing the full setup in a single day.

What happens if the property sells before the lease ends?

Congratulations! Upon escrow ratification and contingency waivers, simply notify us 48 hours in advance, and our white-glove team will promptly de-stage without any hassle.

Can buyers purchase the staging furniture with the home?

Yes! We provide turnkey “as-styled” furniture buyout options directly to interested home buyers through our concierge team.
