Case Study Spotlight

The TriBeCa Loft Transformation

This 4,200 sq. ft. cast-iron loft sat on the market for over 120 days as an empty shell. Buyers struggled to visualize scale and intimacy within the towering 14-foot ceilings.

Spatial Grounding: Custom oversized modular sofas and 12x15 hand-knotted wool rugs anchored conversation zones.
Vertical Harmony: Large-scale sculptural wall tapestries and floor-to-ceiling linen panels balanced loft proportions.
Market Result: Sold within 11 days at full asking price of $6.8M with two competing buyers.
