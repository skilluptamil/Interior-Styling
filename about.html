Our Story

A Passion for Transforming Spaces into Emotional Havens

Founded over a decade ago by a collective of architectural designers and luxury real estate advisors, HAVEN & HEIR was born from a singular insight: buyers do not simply purchase square footage—they purchase the feeling of their future life.

What began as a boutique staging studio has evolved into a full-scale residential presentation powerhouse. Today, we maintain an exclusive multi-million-dollar private collection of designer furnishings, museum-grade art, custom lighting, and bespoke organic textiles.

Whether working with premier property developers, elite listing brokers, or discerning private homeowners, our signature aesthetic merges warm minimalism, tactile luxury, and precise demographic buyer psychology.

Sophia Montgomery
Founder & Principal Creative Director
